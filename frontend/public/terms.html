2. Description of Service

Smart Mailbox Cleaner is a web application that connects to your Gmail account via Google OAuth to:

  • Analyse Gmail message metadata to identify emails consuming storage space
  • Present categorised cleanup suggestions (large attachments, old promotions, etc.)
  • Move selected emails to Gmail Trash at your explicit request
  • Allow you to undo cleanup actions and restore emails within 30 days

The Service never reads, stores, or processes the body or content of your emails. All email access is limited to metadata (size, labels, dates).
